# Saw (2004)

> *"Live or die. Make your choice."* — **Jigsaw**

**Saw** is a 2004 American psychological horror-thriller film directed by *James Wan* in his feature directorial debut, and written by *Leigh Whannell*. It launched one of the highest-grossing horror franchises of all time.

---

## Synopsis

Two strangers, **Adam Stanheight** and **Dr. Lawrence Gordon**, wake up chained to pipes at opposite ends of a dilapidated underground bathroom. Between them lies a dead body holding a revolver and a microcassette recorder. Through recorded instructions, they realize they are the latest victims of the **Jigsaw Killer**, who forces his subjects to endure deadly moral tests to prove their will to survive.

## Key Elements of the Film

* **Non-Linear Storytelling:** Uses flashbacks to slowly reveal how the characters ended up in the bathroom.
* **The Philosophy:** Unlike traditional slashers, Jigsaw believes he never actually kills anyone—he merely tests their survival instincts.
* **Iconic Imagery:** Features `Billy the Puppet`, the reverse bear trap, and gritty, desaturated cinematography.
* **The Twist Ending:** Widely regarded as one of the most shocking endings in modern horror cinema.

## Core Rules of Jigsaw's Game

1. Listen carefully to the instructions on the tape.
2. Follow the rules strictly—cheating leads to immediate consequences.
3. Make a personal sacrifice before the timer runs out (`6:00` on the clock).
